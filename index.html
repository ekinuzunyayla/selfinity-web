What Makes It Different

Puzzle, refined.

How to Play

Three moves, infinite calm.

The rules fit on a napkin. The flow lasts for hours.

1

Place a block

Drag any of the three pieces in your tray onto the 8×8 board. Wherever it fits, it stays.

2

Clear a line

Fill any complete row or column and it vanishes — making room for the next move and the next breath.

3

Chase the combo

Clear lines on consecutive moves to ride the multiplier. Score, rank, and a quiet wave of music reward you.

The Idea

A puzzle that respects your time.

Lotus Blocks borrows the joy of fitting pieces from arcade block-puzzles, but strips away the dark patterns: no pity timers, no rubber-banding difficulty, no pay-to-clear-the-board. Just a fair 8×8 grid, a calm aesthetic, and progression you actually own.

Built by a small team of Kotlin Multiplatform engineers in Türkiye, designed around the Japanese concept of komorebi — sunlight filtered through leaves — and bungu, the quiet beauty of premium stationery.

FAQ

Honest answers.

Is there pay-to-win?
No. Gems (the premium currency) only purchase cosmetics — avatars, themes, music, board frames. The Stars used for power moves can only be earned through gameplay. A free-to-play player can reach the full cosmetic catalogue in about four months of casual play.
How are ads handled?
A short interstitial appears after Game Over with a 60-second cooldown floor. Rewarded ads are fully optional — typically for a daily gem bonus or a one-tap stamp revive. Tutorial play is ad-free.
Is it suitable for children?
Lotus Blocks is rated for a general audience. It contains no violence, no chat, no gambling mechanics, and no behavioural advertising aimed at children. Parents should still supervise in-app purchases per platform guidelines.
What accessibility features are included?
Triple-coded visuals (color + shape + label), full screen-reader semantics on iOS and Android, 48dp minimum touch targets, scalable text, and respect for system Reduce Motion / Reduce Transparency preferences.
Which platforms?
iOS and Android, built with Kotlin Multiplatform and Compose Multiplatform. The game runs locally — no account required for the core experience.
Does it work offline?
Yes. Core gameplay, progression, and cosmetics work fully offline. An internet connection is only needed for in-app purchases, ad delivery, and analytics.
