What is Selfinity?

Selfinity is a comprehensive personal development mobile application designed to help you build better habits, improve mental wellness, and achieve your life goals. Developed by Mindoria Studio, Selfinity combines proven psychological techniques with modern AI technology to provide personalized guidance on your self-improvement journey.

Whether you want to track your mood, journal your thoughts, practice meditation, or monitor daily habits, Selfinity provides all the tools you need in one beautiful, easy-to-use app.

Key Features

📊 Habit Tracking

Build positive daily habits with streak tracking, reminders, and visual progress charts.

📝 Journaling & Thought Records

Keep private journals, mood logs, and cognitive behavioral therapy (CBT) thought records.

🧘 Meditation & Mindfulness

Access guided meditation sessions, breathing exercises, and mindfulness activities.

🤖 AI-Powered Insights

Receive personalized recommendations and motivational messages powered by Google Gemini AI.

🎮 Gamification

Earn rewards, unlock achievements, and level up through consistent engagement.

🎯 Vision Boards

Create visual representations of your goals and aspirations to stay motivated.

😴 Sleep Tracking

Monitor sleep quality and patterns to improve your rest and recovery.

💧 Water Intake Tracker

Stay hydrated with daily water intake tracking and reminders.

👟 Google Health Connect

Sync step count data and integrate with your fitness tracking (optional).
