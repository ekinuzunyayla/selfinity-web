Privacy Policy

Last updated: January 9, 2025

1. Introduction

At Selfinity, we value your privacy. This privacy policy explains what personal information we collect, how we use it, and how we protect it when you use our app.

2. Information We Collect

Our app may collect the following information:

2.1 Account Information

  • Your name and email address when you sign in with Google OAuth
  • Information you provide when registering with email and password
  • Profile picture (optional)

2.2 Usage Data

  • Mood logs and journal entries
  • In-app activities (tasks, habits, meditation sessions)
  • Statistics and progress data

2.3 Device Information

  • Device type and operating system version
  • App usage frequency and duration
  • Error and performance logs

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information for the following purposes:

  • To provide and improve app features
  • To personalize user experience
  • To generate statistics and analytics
  • To provide technical support
  • For security and fraud prevention
  • To comply with legal obligations

4. Data Security

We use industry-standard security measures to protect your data from unauthorized access, modification, disclosure, or destruction:

  • All data is encrypted at rest (AES-256)
  • Secure data transmission with HTTPS
  • Regular security audits
  • Access control mechanisms

5. Data Sharing and Third Parties

We do not share your personal information with third parties. However, we use the following service providers:

  • Supabase: Database and authentication services
  • Google: OAuth authentication
  • AdMob: Advertising (anonymous data)

These service providers use your data only to provide app services and are subject to their own privacy policies.

6. Google OAuth

When you sign in with Google, Google's privacy policy also applies. We only request basic profile information such as your name and email address.

You can review Google's privacy policy here.

7. Data Retention and Deletion

  • Your data is stored as long as your account is active
  • When you delete your account, all personal data is permanently deleted within 30 days
  • You can request deletion of your data at any time

8. Children's Privacy

Our app does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you are under 13, please do not use our app without parental consent.

9. Cookies and Tracking Technologies

Our app stores session information to improve user experience. Anonymous identifiers may be used for ad displays.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. We will notify you of significant changes through in-app notifications. The "Last updated" date will be changed with each update.

11. Your Rights (GDPR & CCPA)

You have the following rights:

  • Request access to your personal data
  • Request correction of your personal data
  • Request deletion of your personal data
  • Object to data processing activities
  • Data portability rights

12. Contact Us

If you have questions or requests regarding our privacy policy, please contact us:

Email: hello@getselfinity.com
App: Selfinity
